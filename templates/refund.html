The ConcertTix Buyer Guarantee

Every order is covered. If your ticket is invalid, never arrives, or the event is cancelled, you get a full refund including the service fee.

1. Cancelled events

If an event is cancelled and not rescheduled, you receive a full refund of the ticket price and the 5% service fee to your original payment method. Refunds are normally processed within 7–10 business days. You can also file a claim from the booking page in My Bookings.

2. Postponed or rescheduled events

Your tickets stay valid for the new date. If you can't attend, request a refund within 14 days of the new date being announced.

3. Invalid or undelivered tickets

If you are refused entry because your QR ticket is invalid, or your e-ticket never appeared in My Tickets, file a Buyer Guarantee claim. We investigate within 2 business days and refund in full if the issue was not caused by you (for example, sharing your QR code with someone else).

4. Change of mind

Tickets are otherwise non-refundable once purchased, as seats are removed from sale for other fans. Please check the date, venue and age limit before you pay.

5. How to claim

  1. Go to My Bookings and open the booking.
  2. Click File a Buyer Guarantee claim, choose a reason and add details.
  3. Our team reviews it and you'll see the decision on the booking page.

6. Fees

ConcertTix charges one service fee: 5% of the ticket subtotal, shown before payment. It is refunded whenever the Buyer Guarantee applies.
