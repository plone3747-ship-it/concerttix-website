Our story

Born from the frustration of buying a ticket.

Live music has moved online, but buying a ticket often hasn't caught up. Fans are sent away from the page to pick a seat, surprised by fees at the very last step, asked to queue for paper tickets, or left with nowhere to turn when a show is cancelled.

ConcertTix started with a simple idea: put everything a fan needs into one clear, trustworthy booking experience, and give organisers the tools to run their shows just as smoothly.

“The price you see is the price you pay. The seat you pick is the seat you get.”
